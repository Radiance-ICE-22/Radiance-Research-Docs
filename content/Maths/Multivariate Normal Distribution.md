## Keywords
- **Marginal distribution** ----> Shows probability of a single variable from a larger set of variables, obtained by "summing out" or ignoring the other variables in a joint distribution, revealing the individual variable's behavior.  (Distribution of one variable)
- **Joint distribution** ---> Distribution of all of them together.
- **Probability Density Function (PDF)**-----> Defines the likelihood of observing a specific point in the multivariate space.
