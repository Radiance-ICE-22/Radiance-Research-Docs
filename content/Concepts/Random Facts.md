Q:What are **Discriminative AI**?
A: **Discriminative AI** is the traditional AI methods learns the **differences** to distinguish between things.  

Take an example of learning to classify speech to languages. 
1. You can learn underlying languages involved and then classify the speech into the languages.
or
2. Determine the **difference** in the lingustic models **without learning the language**, and then classifying the speech.

First one is **generative AI**, second one is **discriminative AI**. 

Discriminative AI learns **conditional probability** in other words  **decision boundaries** in other words, it learns the attributes relative to something else.  Learning $P(Y|X)$.

Generative AI learns the **joint probability**, in other worlds learns the underlying data iteself (expensive process) and has the ability to generate new things. Learning $P(X, Y)$.
