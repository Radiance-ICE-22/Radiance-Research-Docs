A visuomotor policy is a neural network that maps raw visual observations (camera images) directly to robot motor commands (joint positions, end-effector poses, or torques), learning the complete perception-to-action chain as a single differentiable model. The term "visuomotor" combines "visual" (the input modality) with "motor" (the output modality), emphasizing the end-to-end nature of the mapping.
